Installing OxiCut

OxiCut is new, and Windows does not know it yet. That is why it may show a warning. The installer is safe: nothing is installed for other users, and it asks for no special rights.

  1. Download the installer. Your browser may ask whether to keep it. Choose Keep.
  2. Open the file you downloaded.
  3. If a blue window says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. Follow the steps. When it is done, OxiCut opens.

Download for Windows

To remove OxiCut later, use Installed apps in Windows Settings. Your pictures and projects are not deleted.
